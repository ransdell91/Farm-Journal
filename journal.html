Backup & move

Save a copy of your journal, or load one in. Use a backup file to move your records between the Claude page and the phone app.

The backup (.json) can be imported back in. The spreadsheet (.csv) has one row per day and opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Importing never deletes anything: days you don't have are added, and when both copies have a day, the one saved most recently wins.

Your journal saves to your account and syncs across your devices. Ask Claude to fill in a day, change your checklist, or summarize a month any time; it works from the same records.
