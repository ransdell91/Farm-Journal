Backup & move

Save a copy of your journal, or load one in. Use a backup file to move entries between the Claude page and the phone app.

The backup (.json) can be imported back in. The spreadsheet (.csv) opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Importing never deletes anything: new entries are added and newer versions of matching entries replace older ones.

Entries save to your account and sync across your devices. Ask Claude to add, change or summarize entries any time; it works from the same records.
