FARM JOURNAL PHONE APP - SETUP

1. Put this folder online (free):
   GitHub Pages: create a repository, upload every file in this folder
   (keep the icons folder), then Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main > Save.
   Or Netlify Drop: drag this folder onto app.netlify.com/drop and create a free account to keep the site.

2. On your Android phone, open the site address in Chrome.
   Tap the three-dot menu > "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app") > Install.

3. Open Farm Journal from your home screen. It works offline after the first open.

UPDATING
Replace journal.html or plan.html with new versions, and change VERSION in sw.js
(for example farm-journal-v2) so phones pick up the change. Your entries are not affected.

YOUR DATA
Entries are saved on the phone, not on the website. Use Export backup regularly.
Uninstalling the app or clearing Chrome's site data deletes entries that haven't been exported.
